# Tilt Maze

Roll a marble through a random maze by tilting the badge, and race the clock to the gold tile.

## Usage

Each maze starts with a two second "hold steady" count. Hold the badge the way you want to play, the angle at the end of the count is your neutral. Then tilt to roll the marble to the gold tile. The dark tile is the start. Select recentres the tilt at your current angle (and skips the count, and starts the next maze after a win). Each level is a new random maze and gets bigger up to level 4, after that mazes stay the same size. Your best time per level is saved (levels past 4 share the level 4 best). The case lights flash on a win. Back exits.

Uses the onboard accelerometer. The d-pad also rolls the marble, which is how you play in the simulator. Tilt directions have not been checked on a real badge yet. Recentring only moves the neutral angle, it does not flip a direction.
