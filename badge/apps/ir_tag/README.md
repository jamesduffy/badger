# IR Tag

Tag other badges with infrared and see a confirmation when your shot lands.

## Usage

Point the top of your badge at another badge running IR Tag and press Select to fire. A badge that gets hit flashes its case lights red, counts the hit, and sends a confirmation back, so the shooter sees "hit confirmed" and the case lights flash green. Each badge gets an id from its hardware id, shown in the header. Tags landed and hits taken are saved. Back exits.

Needs line of sight, so aim at the other badge's front edge. Uses the IR transmitter and receiver, and needs firmware that provides `aye_arr.nec` with a sender.
